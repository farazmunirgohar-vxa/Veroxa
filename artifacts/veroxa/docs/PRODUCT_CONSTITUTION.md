# Veroxa Product Constitution

## Product identity

Veroxa is an AI-first restaurant growth and online-presence operating system.
Restaurants provide authentic media and owner-confirmed business truth; Veroxa
performs private media intelligence, controlled preparation, review-ready
packaging, reporting, and evidence capture.

## Restaurant responsibility boundary

After a valid upload, the restaurant has no normal operational action. Veroxa
does not ask the restaurant to verify its own bytes, operate AI workflows,
manage queues, diagnose providers, or approve internal processing.

## Truth, media, and lineage

- Originals are immutable and remain associated with the tenant, rights,
  source bytes, object version, and canonical identity.
- Visual observations are private evidence, not restaurant facts.
- Public-facing factual claims require owner-confirmed business truth.
- Unknown business facts remain unknown. Unsupported claims are blocked.

## Authority states

`Ready` means a package has passed applicable deterministic, rights, truth,
lineage, and policy gates and is available for Team review. It is not Team
approval, scheduled, or published.

Team may approve, revise, reject, or discard a Ready package. No internal
decision may create an external write unless a separately authorized external
action is present. External publishing, scheduling, account connection,
messaging, and outreach remain locked for this milestone.

## Operating evidence

Every consequential state change must be attributable to scoped source code,
database state, tests, deployed runtime behavior, and durable audit evidence.
Code or a successful build alone is not production proof.
